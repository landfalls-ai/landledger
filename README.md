# Landledger

The land family's cost subledger: what technology and AI cost, who owns each cost, which spend was approved, and why a
month came out as it did. Built for the CFO team: a daily number with an owner and a reason, approvals with evidence,
and the technology lines explained at month-end. Management reporting (showback); the accounting system and Datarails
stay the books of record.

Clickland measures, Landledger accounts, Landflow acts, Landbright says who, Datarails reports.

Status: **plan and prototype.** Start at [docs/plan.md](docs/plan.md), then [docs/architecture.md](docs/architecture.md)
and [docs/ui.md](docs/ui.md). Open `ui/prototype.html` in a browser for the clickable console.
